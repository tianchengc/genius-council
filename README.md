## 💡 What is Genius Council?

Most AI agents act as passive report generators: you ask a question, and they dump a 5,000-word text essay that you never read or digest.

**Genius Council breaks this cycle.** It transforms your AI agent into an **adversarial multi-mentor war room** that:
1. **Sparrs from 9 distinct worldviews** (East-West philosophical & capital lenses).
2. **Kills naive founder assumptions** before you write a single line of code.
3. **Discovers non-consensus monopolies** that traditional competitors miss.
4. **Delivers Interactive HTML5 War Rooms** equipped with **Audio/Slide Executive Briefings, Live Product Simulators, and $10k/mo MRR Unit Economics Calculators**.

```bash

```
