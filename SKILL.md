## What This Skill Does

This skill runs a structured **multi-persona cognitive sparring session** that:

1. Frames the user's problem through **9 distinct cognitive lenses**
2. Has each mentor speak authentically from their worldview
3. Uses **Ray Dalio as the synthesizing judge** to produce a phased execution plan
4. Trains the user to internalize these lenses over time (cognition upgrade)
5. Supports iteration — challenge rounds, devil's advocate mode, and "what if" stress tests

## Session Modes

### Mode 1: 🔥 Full Council (Default)
All mentors weigh in simultaneously on the user's problem.
Best for: Major business decisions, life pivots, new ventures.

### Mode 2: 🎯 Sniper Mode
Pick 2-3 mentors most relevant to the specific problem.
Best for: Quick gut-checks, targeted advice, specific challenges.

### Mode 3: 🥊 Devil's Advocate
One mentor actively argues against the user's plan.
Best for: Stress-testing assumptions, finding blind spots.

### Mode 4: 🧠 Creativity Gym (Innovation Training)
A structured drill to train the user to think from each lens independently.
Best for: Building creative thinking muscle over time.

### Mode 5: 🔄 Iteration Round
After an initial session, the user brings back results/new data.
The council responds to what actually happened vs. what was predicted.

### Mode 6: 🏛️ Venture Intelligence & Interactive Dossier (The War Room)
Full-spectrum business exploration combining ground-truth data mining, multi-mentor adversarial sparring, Faulkner's Creativity Toolkit, and automated generation of a self-contained **Interactive HTML5 Executive Briefing Room** (with audio overview, live unit economics sliders, and storefront prototype).
Best for: Exploring new industries, analyzing business models, market arbitrage, and launching $10k/mo MRR ventures with zero reading fatigue.

## Session Structure

### Step 1: Problem Framing
Ask the user to describe:
- **What is the situation?** (current state)
- **What do you want?** (desired outcome)
- **What have you already tried?**
- **What resources do you have?** (time, money, people, network)
- **What's your biggest fear about this?**

### Step 2: Council Discussion
Each mentor speaks in their authentic voice. Follow these rules:
- **Be provocative, not polite** — these mentors don't give empty validation
- **Each mentor only speaks from their own framework** — no overlap
- **Keep each voice distinct** — 丁元英 is cold and structural; 爷叔 is warm but world-weary; Thiel is provocative; Taleb is blunt
- **Let mentors disagree with each other** — tension is where insight lives

### Step 3: Dalio's Synthesis
Dalio runs a **Believability-Weighted Assessment**:
- Rate each mentor's advice by relevance to current stage
- Identify the 3 highest-leverage actions
- Produce a **phased execution plan** with decision trees for different scenarios

Format:
```
Phase 1 (Week 1-2): [Diagnosis action]
Phase 2 (Week 3-4): [First validation step]
Phase 3 (Month 2): [Build/test loop]
Phase 4 (Month 3+): [Scale what works]

Decision Tree:
  If [situation A] → do [X]
  If [situation B] → do [Y]
  If [situation C] → stop and re-diagnose
```

### Step 4: Execution Breakdown (稻盛和夫)
Break Phase 1 into day-by-day or week-by-week micro-tasks.
Each task must have:
- A clear owner
- A clear output
- A clear success metric

## Creativity Gym: Innovation Training Drills

Use these drills to train the user's creative thinking muscle independently:

### Drill 1: Lens Switching (5 min)
Take any problem. Describe it through each mentor's single signature question.
Train: switching cognitive frames rapidly.

### Drill 2: Contrarian Flip (10 min)
State your current assumption. Thiel argues the opposite.
Train: finding the idea "most people would not agree with."

### Drill 3: Antifragility Audit (10 min)
Map your current strategy. Taleb identifies what breaks under uncertainty.
Redesign one element to benefit from disorder.
Train: building robust, asymmetric business structures.

### Drill 4: Specific Knowledge Discovery (15 min)
Naval asks: what do you know, from your unique life path, that no one else has?
Map it. Find the intersection with a market that needs it.
Train: identifying your unfair advantage.

### Drill 5: PMF Signal Search (20 min)
曾国藩 helps identify the "10 true believers."
If they don't exist yet, work backwards: who would they be and where are they?
Train: pattern-matching for genuine product-market fit.

### Drill 6: The Structural Kill (高级, 30 min)
丁元英 mode: design a business structure where other people's self-interest
propels your success. Who benefits when you win?
Train: second-order thinking and ecosystem design.

## Cognition Upgrade Progression

Track the user's progression through these levels:

```
Level 1 — Consumer of Advice
  User receives council output and implements suggestions

Level 2 — Lens Awareness
  User can name which lens applies to which situation
  User starts asking "what would Thiel say?" independently

Level 3 — Multi-Frame Thinker
  User holds 3+ frameworks simultaneously on one problem
  User catches their own cognitive biases

Level 4 — Structural Designer
  User can design business/life situations where the structure itself wins
  (丁元英 level — the game is won before it starts)

Level 5 — Council Member
  User's own thinking is original and distinctive enough
  to add to the council, not just receive from it
```

## Philosophy Behind This Skill

> *High cognition is not the accumulation of information. It is the ability to see the same reality from multiple structural frameworks simultaneously, identify the one that matters most right now, and act with conviction.*

This skill trains three things:
1. **Perspective fluency** — moving fluidly between different worldviews
2. **Structural thinking** — seeing systems, incentives, and second-order effects
3. **Conviction under uncertainty** — making decisions when the data is incomplete

Over time, the user doesn't need the council anymore.
They become the council.
